# Roadmap

## Where todouz is going

todouz is a personal life OS for the age of AI agents. It does three things well:

1. **Plan.** A calm place for tasks, lists and a real schedule.
2. **Remember.** One context layer (profile, goals, decisions, learnings) that every AI you use reads first.
3. **Scroll.** A feed your AI writes for you, so checking your phone moves your life forward instead of eating it.

It stays personal and local-first: plain markdown you own, one person or a household, no team features.

## Now: MVP (v0.2)

- Today, calendar (day, month, year), lists, board, item drawer with checklist and sessions
- Brain with profile, roadmap and typed notes; "what your AI knows about you" overview
- Feed with daily brief, one-tap triage, steer bar ("tell your feed what you want"), reactions, share-a-link
- MCP server and HTTP API (74 tools), in-app Ask agent, accounts, push reminders, PWA
- Demo workspace, Docker, opt-in routines

## Next

- **Onboarding interview.** First run asks five questions and writes your profile and roadmap.
- **Calendar sync.** Read Google Calendar / ICS so meetings show up next to your sessions.
- **Feed sources.** RSS, newsletters and saved links as raw material for the feed, filtered by your Brain.
- **Mobile share sheet.** Share a reel, article or screenshot from your phone straight into todouz.
- **Weekly review in the feed.** A swipeable review of what got done, what slipped and what to drop.

## Later

- Natural-language quick add everywhere ("gym tue thu 7am for 6 weeks")
- Local models for routines (Ollama) so the feed can run without a cloud plan
- Import from Todoist, Notion and Obsidian
- Desktop app wrapper

## Cut from the MVP

The Brain "nebula" star map, the Cradle and the speaking-practice journey were fun experiments, but they don't serve the three jobs above, so they're out of this release.
